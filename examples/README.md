# The Inland Compass

This short fictional EPUB was written for Spoiless and is dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It may be shared and reused. It contains six deliberately short chapters; the first two share a single EPUB document to exercise chapter boundaries inside a document. Its chapter titles are generic and its characters and events are fictional.

The text, sample EPUB generator, authored questions, and expected evidence are in `scripts/evaluate_retrieval.py`. This tiny synthetic example supports repeatable checks; it is not a representative measure of retrieval quality across real books.
